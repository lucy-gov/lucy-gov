I work at the intersection of data governance, digital service delivery, and public-sector technology.

I'm a Data Governance Strategist with [CalData](https://innovation.ca.gov/who-we-are/caldata/) at the [California Office of Data and Innovation](https://innovation.ca.gov/), where I create, translate, and implement data and artificial intelligence governance standards as practical guidance, reusable tools, and measurable practices that departments can actually adopt. I care about government technology that works for the people it serves and that strengthens transparency, accountability, and democracy.

## What I work on

- 🗂️ **Data governance standards and implementation** including metadata, lineage, data quality, access controls, reference models, playbooks, and adoption metrics
- 🏛️ **Digital service delivery** that is user-centered, effective, and iteratively developed
- 🔒 **Privacy, security, and responsible data use** as everyday implementation practices, not afterthoughts
- 🤖 **Responsible AI in government** including practical guidance for departments adopting AI tools, particularly generative AI
- 🧑‍🏫 **Training, coaching, and communities of practice** that help departmental data and IT teams learn from each other
- 🌐 **Working in the open** through open-source code, public documentation, and reusable templates

## Working principles

I care about code, data systems, and public services that are:

- **User-centered** — designed around the people governments serve and the staff who deliver services, and useful for real decisions
- **Transparent and accountable** — methods, data use, and decisions are visible, explainable, and protective of privacy and public trust
- **Reproducible and reusable** — others can rerun, review, and build on the work
- **Maintainable** — projects can survive beyond one person or one deadline
- **Accessible** — tools and documentation are approachable for new learners and straightforward to use for people with diverse access needs
- **Joyful** — workflows are designed to be satisfying, welcoming, and even fun to use
